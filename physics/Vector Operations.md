# Vector Operations

## Equal and Opposite Vectors

- **Equal vectors**: same magnitude, same direction, same sense.
- **Opposite vectors**: same magnitude, same direction, **opposite** sense.

## Addition of Collinear Vectors

### Same direction and sense

$$|\vec{R}| = |\vec{u}| + |\vec{v}|$$

The resultant $\vec{R}$ has the same direction and sense as $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.

### Same direction, opposite sense

$$|\vec{R}| = \bigl||\vec{u}| - |\vec{v}|\bigr|$$

The resultant $\vec{R}$ has the same direction and the sense of the vector with greater magnitude.

## Properties of Vector Addition

**Commutative law:**

$$\vec{u} + \vec{v} = \vec{v} + \vec{u}$$

**Associative law:**

$$(\vec{u} + \vec{v}) + \vec{w} = \vec{u} + (\vec{v} + \vec{w})$$

## Addition of Two Vectors at an Angle — Law of Cosines (Parallelogram Method)

Given two vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ forming an angle $\alpha$ between them, the magnitude of the resultant $\vec{R}$ is:

$$|\vec{R}| = \sqrt{|\vec{u}|^2 + |\vec{v}|^2 + 2\,|\vec{u}|\,|\vec{v}|\,\cos\alpha}$$

where:
- $|\vec{u}|$ — magnitude of vector $\vec{u}$
- $|\vec{v}|$ — magnitude of vector $\vec{v}$
- $\alpha$ — angle between $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$

### Special case — Perpendicular vectors ($\alpha = 90°$)

Since $\cos 90° = 0$, the formula reduces to the **Pythagorean theorem**:

$$|\vec{R}| = \sqrt{|\vec{u}|^2 + |\vec{v}|^2}$$

## Vector Difference

Given two vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ forming an angle $\alpha$, the magnitude of the difference vector $\vec{V}_d = \vec{u} - \vec{v}$ is:

$$|\vec{V}_d| = \sqrt{|\vec{u}|^2 + |\vec{v}|^2 - 2\,|\vec{u}|\,|\vec{v}|\,\cos\alpha}$$

where:
- The direction is along $\overrightarrow{VU}$ (from the tip of $\vec{v}$ to the tip of $\vec{u}$)
- The sense is from $V$ to $U$

## Scalar Multiplication

Multiplying a vector $\vec{u}$ by a scalar $k$:

$$\vec{R} = k\,\vec{u}$$

- The magnitude is scaled: $|\vec{R}| = |k| \cdot |\vec{u}|$
- The direction remains the same
- If $k > 0$, the sense is preserved; if $k < 0$, the sense is reversed
