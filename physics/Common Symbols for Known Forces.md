
| Force | Symbol | Direction | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravity (Weight) | $\vec{F}_G$ or $\vec{w}$ | Downward (toward center of Earth) | Long-range force acting on every object with mass. Magnitude: $F_G = mg$ |
| Spring Force | $\vec{F}_{sp}$ | Opposite to displacement from equilibrium | Contact force from a compressed or stretched spring. Magnitude: $F_{sp} = k \Delta s$ (Hooke's Law) |
| Tension | $\vec{T}$ | Along the rope/string, away from the object | Pull force transmitted through a rope, string, or cable. Same magnitude at both ends for massless rope. |
| Normal Force | $\vec{n}$ | Perpendicular to the contact surface, away from it | Contact force exerted by a surface against an object pressing on it |
| Static Friction | $\vec{f}_s$ | Along the surface, opposite to the direction the object would move if there were no friction | Contact force that prevents an object from sliding. Magnitude: $f_s \leq \mu_s n$ |
| Kinetic Friction | $\vec{f}_k$ | Along the surface, opposite to the direction of motion | Contact force on a sliding object. Magnitude: $f_k = \mu_k n$ |
| Drag | $\vec{F}_{drag}$ or $\vec{D}$ | Opposite to the direction of motion | Resistive force from a fluid (air or liquid). Magnitude depends on speed, size, and shape. |
| Thrust | $\vec{F}_{thrust}$ | Direction of the exhaust (reaction force) | Propulsion force from a jet or rocket engine (Newton's 3rd law) |

## Notes

- **Contact forces** require physical touch between objects: normal, friction, tension, spring, drag, thrust.
- **Long-range forces** act without contact: gravity (and later, electric and magnetic forces).
