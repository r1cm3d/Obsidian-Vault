# Thermal Power (Heat Rate)

**Thermal power** is the rate at which thermal energy (heat) is transferred or converted, measured in watts (W).

## Thermal Power

$$P = \frac{Q}{t}$$

where:
- $P$ — thermal power $[\text{W}]$
- $Q$ — quantity of heat transferred $[\text{J}]$
- $t$ — time $[\text{s}]$

> $1\ \text{W} = 1\ \text{J/s}$

## Heat Capacity

The **heat capacity** (or thermal capacity) of a body is the amount of heat required to raise its temperature by one degree:

$$C = \frac{Q}{\Delta T}$$

where:
- $C$ — heat capacity $[\text{J/°C}]$ or $[\text{cal/°C}]$
- $Q$ — heat absorbed or released $[\text{J}]$ or $[\text{cal}]$
- $\Delta T$ — temperature change $[\text{°C}]$ or $[\text{K}]$

## Energy Conversion

$$1\ \text{cal} = 4.2\ \text{J} \qquad 1\ \text{J} = \frac{1}{4.2}\ \text{cal} \approx 0.238\ \text{cal}$$
