# Physical Quantities — International System of Units (SI) & Conversions

---

## 1. SI Quantities

### 1.1 Base Quantities

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Length | $l$ | metre | $\mathrm{m}$ | $\mathsf{L}$ |
| Mass | $m$ | kilogram | $\mathrm{kg}$ | $\mathsf{M}$ |
| Time | $t$ | second | $\mathrm{s}$ | $\mathsf{T}$ |
| Electric current | $I$ | ampere | $\mathrm{A}$ | $\mathsf{I}$ |
| Temperature | $T$ | kelvin | $\mathrm{K}$ | $\Theta$ |
| Amount of substance | $n$ | mole | $\mathrm{mol}$ | $\mathsf{N}$ |
| Luminous intensity | $I_v$ | candela | $\mathrm{cd}$ | $\mathsf{J}$ |

### 1.2 Mechanics

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Area | $A$ | square metre | $\mathrm{m^{2}}$ | $\mathsf{L^{2}}$ |
| Volume | $V$ | cubic metre | $\mathrm{m^{3}}$ | $\mathsf{L^{3}}$ |
| Velocity | $v$ | metre per second | $\mathrm{m/s}$ | $\mathsf{L \cdot T^{-1}}$ |
| Acceleration | $a$ | metre per second squared | $\mathrm{m/s^{2}}$ | $\mathsf{L \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Force | $F$ | newton | $\mathrm{N}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Pressure | $p$ | pascal | $\mathrm{Pa}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{-1} \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Energy / Work | $E,\; W$ | joule | $\mathrm{J}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Power | $P$ | watt | $\mathrm{W}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-3}}$ |
| Linear momentum | $p$ | kilogram metre per second | $\mathrm{kg \cdot m/s}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L \cdot T^{-1}}$ |
| Density | $\rho$ | kilogram per cubic metre | $\mathrm{kg/m^{3}}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{-3}}$ |
| Frequency | $f$ | hertz | $\mathrm{Hz}$ | $\mathsf{T^{-1}}$ |

### 1.3 Electromagnetism

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electric charge | $q$ | coulomb | $\mathrm{C}$ | $\mathsf{I \cdot T}$ |
| Electric potential | $V$ | volt | $\mathrm{V}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-3} \cdot I^{-1}}$ |
| Electric resistance | $R$ | ohm | $\Omega$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-3} \cdot I^{-2}}$ |
| Capacitance | $C$ | farad | $\mathrm{F}$ | $\mathsf{M^{-1} \cdot L^{-2} \cdot T^{4} \cdot I^{2}}$ |
| Inductance | $L$ | henry | $\mathrm{H}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-2} \cdot I^{-2}}$ |
| Magnetic field | $B$ | tesla | $\mathrm{T}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot T^{-2} \cdot I^{-1}}$ |
| Magnetic flux | $\Phi$ | weber | $\mathrm{Wb}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-2} \cdot I^{-1}}$ |
| Conductance | $G$ | siemens | $\mathrm{S}$ | $\mathsf{M^{-1} \cdot L^{-2} \cdot T^{3} \cdot I^{2}}$ |

### 1.4 Thermodynamics

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heat / Thermal energy | $Q$ | joule | $\mathrm{J}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Entropy | $S$ | joule per kelvin | $\mathrm{J/K}$ | $\mathsf{M \cdot L^{2} \cdot T^{-2} \cdot \Theta^{-1}}$ |
| Specific heat capacity | $c$ | joule per kilogram kelvin | $\mathrm{J/(kg \cdot K)}$ | $\mathsf{L^{2} \cdot T^{-2} \cdot \Theta^{-1}}$ |

### 1.5 Optics / Radiometry

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luminous flux | $\Phi_v$ | lumen | $\mathrm{lm}$ | $\mathsf{J}$ |
| Illuminance | $E_v$ | lux | $\mathrm{lx}$ | $\mathsf{J \cdot L^{-2}}$ |
| Plane angle | $\theta$ | radian | $\mathrm{rad}$ | dimensionless |
| Solid angle | $\Omega$ | steradian | $\mathrm{sr}$ | dimensionless |

### 1.6 Radioactivity

| Quantity | Symbol | SI Unit | SI Symbol | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radioactivity | $A$ | becquerel | $\mathrm{Bq}$ | $\mathsf{T^{-1}}$ |
| Absorbed dose | $D$ | gray | $\mathrm{Gy}$ | $\mathsf{L^{2} \cdot T^{-2}}$ |
| Equivalent dose | $H$ | sievert | $\mathrm{Sv}$ | $\mathsf{L^{2} \cdot T^{-2}}$ |

## 2. Conversion Tables by Quantity Type

### 2.1 Length → $\mathrm{m}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| kilometre | $\mathrm{km}$ | $\times 10^{3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{km} = 1\,000\;\mathrm{m}$ |
| centimetre | $\mathrm{cm}$ | $\times 10^{-2}$ | $1\;\mathrm{cm} = 0.01\;\mathrm{m}$ |
| millimetre | $\mathrm{mm}$ | $\times 10^{-3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{mm} = 0.001\;\mathrm{m}$ |
| micrometre | $\mathrm{\mu m}$ | $\times 10^{-6}$ | $1\;\mathrm{\mu m} = 10^{-6}\;\mathrm{m}$ |
| nanometre | $\mathrm{nm}$ | $\times 10^{-9}$ | $1\;\mathrm{nm} = 10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m}$ |
| inch | $\mathrm{in}$ | $\times 0.0254$ | $1\;\mathrm{in} = 2.54\;\mathrm{cm}$ |
| foot | $\mathrm{ft}$ | $\times 0.3048$ | $1\;\mathrm{ft} = 30.48\;\mathrm{cm}$ |
| mile | $\mathrm{mi}$ | $\times 1{,}609.34$ | $1\;\mathrm{mi} = 1.609\;\mathrm{km}$ |
| ångström | $\mathrm{\AA}$ | $\times 10^{-10}$ | $1\;\mathrm{\AA} = 0.1\;\mathrm{nm}$ |
| light-year | $\mathrm{ly}$ | $\times 9.461 \times 10^{15}$ | $1\;\mathrm{ly} \approx 9.46 \times 10^{15}\;\mathrm{m}$ |

### 2.2 Mass → $\mathrm{kg}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| gram | $\mathrm{g}$ | $\times 10^{-3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{g} = 0.001\;\mathrm{kg}$ |
| milligram | $\mathrm{mg}$ | $\times 10^{-6}$ | $1\;\mathrm{mg} = 10^{-6}\;\mathrm{kg}$ |
| tonne | $\mathrm{t}$ | $\times 10^{3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{t} = 1\,000\;\mathrm{kg}$ |
| pound | $\mathrm{lb}$ | $\times 0.4536$ | $1\;\mathrm{lb} \approx 453.6\;\mathrm{g}$ |
| ounce | $\mathrm{oz}$ | $\times 0.02835$ | $1\;\mathrm{oz} \approx 28.35\;\mathrm{g}$ |
| atomic mass unit | $\mathrm{u}$ | $\times 1.661 \times 10^{-27}$ | $1\;\mathrm{u} \approx 1.661 \times 10^{-27}\;\mathrm{kg}$ |

### 2.3 Time → $\mathrm{s}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| millisecond | $\mathrm{ms}$ | $\times 10^{-3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{ms} = 0.001\;\mathrm{s}$ |
| microsecond | $\mathrm{\mu s}$ | $\times 10^{-6}$ | $1\;\mathrm{\mu s} = 10^{-6}\;\mathrm{s}$ |
| minute | $\mathrm{min}$ | $\times 60$ | $1\;\mathrm{min} = 60\;\mathrm{s}$ |
| hour | $\mathrm{h}$ | $\times 3{,}600$ | $1\;\mathrm{h} = 3{,}600\;\mathrm{s}$ |
| day | $\mathrm{d}$ | $\times 86{,}400$ | $1\;\mathrm{d} = 86{,}400\;\mathrm{s}$ |
| year | $\mathrm{a}$ | $\times 3.156 \times 10^{7}$ | $1\;\mathrm{a} \approx 3.156 \times 10^{7}\;\mathrm{s}$ |

### 2.4 Velocity → $\mathrm{m/s}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| km/h | $\mathrm{km/h}$ | $\div 3.6$ | $1\;\mathrm{km/h} \approx 0.2778\;\mathrm{m/s}$ |
| mph | $\mathrm{mph}$ | $\times 0.4470$ | $1\;\mathrm{mph} \approx 0.447\;\mathrm{m/s}$ |
| knot | $\mathrm{kn}$ | $\times 0.5144$ | $1\;\mathrm{kn} \approx 0.514\;\mathrm{m/s}$ |
| speed of light | $c$ | $\times 2.998 \times 10^{8}$ | $c \approx 3 \times 10^{8}\;\mathrm{m/s}$ |

### 2.5 Force → $\mathrm{N}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |

### 2.6 Pressure → $\mathrm{Pa}$

### 2.7 Energy → $\mathrm{J}$

|---|---|---|---|

### 2.8 Power → $\mathrm{W}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|
| metric horsepower | $\mathrm{cv}$ | $\times 735.499$ | $1\;\mathrm{cv} \approx 735.5\;\mathrm{W}$ |
| horsepower | $\mathrm{hp}$ | $\times 745.700$ | $1\;\mathrm{hp} \approx 745.7\;\mathrm{W}$ |
| kilowatt | $\mathrm{kW}$ | $\times 10^{3}$ | $1\;\mathrm{kW} = 1{,}000\;\mathrm{W}$ |

### 2.9 Temperature → $\mathrm{K}$

| Unit | Symbol | Conversion → SI | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Celsius | $\mathrm{°C}$ | $K = \mathrm{°C} + 273.15$ | $0\;\mathrm{°C} = 273.15\;\mathrm{K}$ |
| Fahrenheit | $\mathrm{°F}$ | $K = (\mathrm{°F} + 459.67) \;/\; 1.8$ | $32\;\mathrm{°F} = 0\;\mathrm{°C} = 273.15\;\mathrm{K}$ |
| Rankine | $\mathrm{°R}$ | $K = \mathrm{°R} \;/\; 1.8$ | $491.67\;\mathrm{°R} = 273.15\;\mathrm{K}$ |

### 2.10 Volume → $\mathrm{m^{3}}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
|---|---|---|---|

| gallon (US) | $\mathrm{gal}$ | $\times 0.003785$ | $1\;\mathrm{gal} \approx 3.785\;\mathrm{L}$ |

### 2.11 Angle → $\mathrm{rad}$

### 2.12 Electric Charge → $\mathrm{C}$

| Unit | Symbol | Factor → SI | Equivalence |
