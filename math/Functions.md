# Functions

### 1. Mapping Types
- **Injective (One-to-One):** $f(x_1) = f(x_2) \implies x_1 = x_2$
- **Surjective (Onto):** $\text{Range}(f) = \text{Codomain}$
- **Bijective:** Both injective and surjective (Admits Inverse $f^{-1}$)

### 2. Parity and Symmetry
- **Even Function:** $f(x) = f(-x)$ (Symmetric to Y-axis)
- **Odd Function:** $f(-x) = -f(x)$ (Symmetric to Origin)

### 3. Graph Shifting and Transformations ($k > 0$)
- **Vertical Shift:** $f(x) \pm k$ (Up/Down)
- **Horizontal Shift:** $f(x \mp k)$ (Right for $-k$, Left for $+k$)
- **Reflections:** $-f(x)$ (X-axis reflection), $f(-x)$ (Y-axis reflection)
